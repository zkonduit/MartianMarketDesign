# Martian Market

## Design Fiction
Martian Market is an exercise in Design Fiction. 
You may think of design fiction as the experimental cousin of Science Fiction.
The goal of Design Fiction is to simulate speculative futures using prototypes and exploring "what-ifs".
In this particular case we want to understand how economies could evolve suppose if humans were to reimagine a new economic framework for Mars.

## The Setting
It's the year 2048. 
In this particular launch window, 
humans have finally succesfully colonized Mars to setup its initial colony. 
Martian weather is dry, cold, windy and more radioactive than Earth. 
Dust storms are frequent occurences.
The hostile weather neccessitates that most laborous outdoor work are usually managed through Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.
While automation had ameliorated the need for labor, the inaccessibility of raw minerals makes any concept of post-scarcity a distant concept.
More intelligent means of coordinating good and services is required.
