# Martian Market

## Design Fiction
Martian Market is an exercise in Design Fiction. 
You may think of design fiction as the experimental cousin of Science Fiction.
The goal of Design Fiction is to simulate speculative futures using prototypes and exploring "what-ifs".
In this particular setting we would like to explore what a new growing Martian settlement would be like.
Participants join a growing Martian settlement, develop relationships, negotiate exchanges, and influence the evolution of the settlement.

We begin with things people can actually offer: transportation, goods, lodging, shared meals. Then account for the history of these exchanges within vector embeddings.

[martian.market](https://martian.market) is the project's existing prototype.

The following settings supplies a context and shared history of the martian colony.
Participants can change is future through their actions.

## The Setting

### Early Martians Expeditions

Martian expeditions have been occuring in the early 2040s. 
By 2048, several dozen people, approaching hundreds to a thousand started arriving on Mars.
The initial Martian settlement started growing through interconnected habitats. 
Initial space-agency missions surveyed sites. Arcadia Planitia was selected.
Arcadaia Planitia has relatively flat terrain and is lower in elevation, providing additional radiation shielding. 
Some ice deposits were located below, trapped in crystals or as frozen ice.
Each new generation of settlers landing on Mars brought new instruments and built more temporary outposts. Some expeditions were government funded, whilst most were funded through private equity investments in space. 
The journey to Mars remained perilous and infrequent (roughly every 26 months) when Earth approaches Mars.
Some expeditions failed catastrophically with a 15% failure rate per mission due to equipment failures due to radiation-induced embrittlement. 
Relativistic micrometeroids while rare, caused a serious damage to a manned space mission, causing the entire crew of the 2042 space mission to perish.
A manned mission to return to Earth was not yet feasible, every manned mission to Mars was a one way trip until more infrastructure on Mars is built.

While Robots have been introduced in the early Martian colony, maintenance of such robots still remained dependent on people, solar energy, and replacement parts.
Extraction of Martian minerals remained an engineering feat, as there were still limited extraction and processing capabilities beyond smaller scale research prototypes.
Duststorms and the constant bombardment from radiation threatened the survivability of electronics and machinery in the long term. [NASA on Martian dust](https://www.nasa.gov/solar-system/the-fact-and-fiction-of-martian-dust-storms/).

### Earth Charter Association

An Earth Charter Association (EAC) built a community on Earth before most members could travel.
The financiers of the private Martian expeditions remain largely on Earth until the return mission from Mars became viable. 
The association targeted possible residents who could be useful on the Martian expeditions: scientists, technicians, doctors, agronomists, pilots.
These experts, generally poorer than the financiers, made expeditions to Mars possible.
The Association issued contracts, offering wages for families on earth, future ownership and property rights on Mars, providing a gateway to generational wealth on Martian soil.

Mars remains a contentious political point of discussion between the quasi-sovereign Earth Charter Association and existing nation states on Earth. Agencies remember the public monies used to fund the expeditions, Financier remember taking the immense financial risks with each expedition. The Earth Charter Association community members and early Martian settlers remember the perilous journey and the risks to their lives they took to make the Mars colony possible.
Everyone desires sovereignty and power to determine the future of Mars and its founding authority. Martian independence remained a contested
legal and diplomatic development. [Outer Space Treaty, Articles II, VI and VIII](https://www.nasa.gov/history/SP-4225/documentation/cooperation/treaty.htm) [Jamestown history](https://www.nps.gov/jame/learn/historyculture/a-short-history-of-jamestown.htm)

### The Earth that was left behind

Climate catastrophe had rendered Earth more hostile than usual. While still habitable, deep inequalities had been produced due to the exponential differences in the rate of capital appreciation and income generated through labor. 
Climate damage, deforestation, water shortages, financial pressures, and regional conflicts are intertwined with restoration projects and prosperous but insulated cities. 
The richer countries were able to insulate themselves from disruption, but many other countries suffered the brunt of climate catastrophe. 
Many on people on Earth remained hopeful for a fix to the past damage, however, the calamities on Earth made Mars an important insurance scheme for the future of humanity.
Mars remains more uninhabitable than Earth.
[IPCC synthesis](https://report.ipcc.ch/ar6syr/headline.html).

The EAC was established largely as a reaction by the new technological saavy elites to contest the pre-existing monetary system.
They particularly criticize the US fiscal policy and dollar dominance. However, the EAC are without its opponents, as their critics believe them to be a dangerous challenger to the existing institutions.

The US dollar itself emerged through institutional changes. Starting with the establishement of national banknotes and the Federal Reserve. The Bretten Woods ended the official dollar to gold convertibility. [Federal Reserve history](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/federal-reserve-history),
[Bretton Woods history](https://www.federalreservehistory.org/essays/bretton-woods-launched). 
Foreign countries to the US had been trying to derisk away from the USD given a spectacular sovereign wealth default that happened in the 2030s resulting in a multi-year depression.
The need for new standards had resulted in countries leaning back towards commodity currencies like gold, and more experimental cryptocurrencies.
The grievances against the US dominance rose in the 2030s. Asymmetric warfare like bio-weapons and drones, made the US military might something to be challenged. [IMF on dollar spillovers](https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2022/10/14/how-countries-should-respond-to-the-strong-dollar).

The Martian settlement offers a new experimental ground in sovereignty like never before. While Earth were still largely beholden to Earth currencies, and Martian settlements beholden to Earth currencies for the purchase of equipment and financing, there was a need for a local coordination mechanism. Martian exports remained an emerging industry, so much of the Martian economy was built on settlement coordination.

### The Martian Economy

Initially, most Martian settlers knew one another. They remember the favors, competence, quirks, and sacrifices made by fellow Martian settlers. The initial settlement coordinated through goodwill and favors. However, as the Martian settlement grew beyond a hundred, approaching to a thousand, it became hard to cognitively keep track of obligations and favors. [Lindenfors, Wartel, and Lind](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8103230/).

Newer recruits and settlers changed the societal dynamics of the Martian settlement.
Early Martians remember the close knit ties they had with other Martians. However, the close knit nature of the Martian colony started dissipating with the given years and growth.
The growth provided benefits and tensions. Talented newcomers may provide skills and expertise to the settlement but were less reputable compared to earlier Martian settlers.
The new arrivals challenged the unwritten rules and status quo of the Martian settlement.

The friction in coordination led to the Martian colony in deciding that a new kind of coordination mechanism is needed. Hence, the Martian Market was created.

### Martian Market

Given the relative abundance of compute already on Mars and adventurous nature of the early Martians, there was desire to build a system that breaks way from Earthly economies.

The scalar 1 dimensional nature of money served as a primitive historical artifact. With computers there is little reason to compress values and meaning into a single dimension. 
In a way money served as a crude dimensionality reduction process which may lead to negative externalities. 
Earth's climate was ruined due to misalignment of interests. Environment, Social, Government (ESG) goals ended up being suggestions rather as genuine goals.
Corporations still had to maximize shareholder value which was accounted in a single dimensional money on their balance sheets.

**What if money encoded more meaning?**

The central hypothesis of the Martian market is that transferable learned vectors can serve as money.
Money may have different dimensions and considerations. The money that arises from deforestation has different implications from money that was earned through regenerative projects.
Reputation, provenance, ethical considerations, remains an important role in the nature of money. 

Kocherlakota's account of money as memory motivates recording economic history
without proving that a learned vector currency will work. Remembering an
exchange also leaves questions about relevance, interpretation, and forgiveness.[Money as memory](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/research/quarterly-review/the-technological-role-of-fiat-money).
Encoding the entire history of money for all participants and accounting between directed acyclic graphs may be computationally costly over many participants. Vector embeddings may serve as a resonable representation of history. 

For participant $p$, we represent a balance as

$$
b_p \in \mathbb{R}^{d}.
$$

We may encode arbitrary amount of dimensions to the money, the transformation of the vector balances are subject to a AI model whose goal is to maximize monetary velocity and certain desirable vector projections.

For example, a climate projection could be defined as

$$
s_{\mathrm{climate}}(b_p;c,t)=w_{\mathrm{climate}}(c,t)^\top b_p,
$$

where $c$ records relevant context and $t$ time.
Other projections describe energy, repair, or estimated USD value. Projection value require calibration with a reference dataset.
Such projections can be obtained through an autoencoder.

To maximize the utility of the Martian settlement, an optimizer can search over offer and wants and payment vectors satisfying
inventories, availability, deadlines, acceptance conditions, and agreed limits on resulting positions. 
It can expose competing objectives and Pareto-efficient alternatives. Choosing among those alternatives still requires preferences or collective rules. 
Physical deliveries, disputed quality, transaction risk, and consent can be encoded in the vector dimensions and be part of settlement. [Boyd and Vandenberghe](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/).

### Open Questions
- Can learned vector balances remain understandable and acceptable as money?
- Who may issue money, authorize credit, or change monetary interpretations as the community grows? Can it be entirely driven through an optimizer? 
- Can more data, compute, and better optimization techniques allow us to revisit centrally planned economies? In a way the economy right now is a slow optimization process.
- How much memory can vector embeddings effectively encode? Do we need a better mechanism for memory? How can migrations to future mathematical frameworks be done fairly for all participants
- Can disputes be minimized?
